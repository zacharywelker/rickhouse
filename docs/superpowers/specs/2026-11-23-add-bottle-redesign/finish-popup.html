Add finish: the popup, and the Finish row behind it (clickable)

Type a name in the Finish row. Pick an existing finish, or choose “Add details…” for the popup. The row behind it follows your pairing rules: months, which mashbill, a share only when there is something to share, an unfinished portion, and a total of 100. The demo bar changes the spirit (finish or cask type) and how many mashbills the label has.

What changed, and what stayed
  • Kept: a finish or cask type has a Name (required), a Type (required: Wood, Wine, Fortified, Beer, Spirit, Other) and Notes. It's reusable, so a Port you make once is offered on every label. The title and the table heading say “Cask type” for Canadian, Irish and Scotch whisky, amaro and liqueur, as today.
  • Type is a row of toggle chips, one on, with a line under it saying what the chosen type means (my wording). It was a dropdown.
  • My proposal: the type is suggested from the name (“oak” suggests Wood, “port” or “sherry” Fortified, “cognac” Spirit, and so on). It shows as a chip marked “suggested” until you choose one yourself. Create “…” (name only) uses the same suggestion. Today it files the finish as “Other”.
  • Warnings are in the footer, left of Cancel, in the same amber style: a missing name or type, and a name that already exists (names are unique, so Save is blocked and “Use it” adds the existing one).
  • No mashbill is preselected when the label has two or more: each finish starts on “Choose…”, with no warning. With one mashbill it is implied.
  • The row behind follows your pairing rules, unchanged: months are optional, a Mashbill column appears with two or more mashbills, a Share column with two or more finishes (or an unfinished portion), a finish on several mashbills gets one line per mashbill with a read-only total, and a total other than 100% is flagged. Mashbill shares are computed from the pairs and shown read-only.
