Add mashbill: the popup in the data-sheet style (clickable)

Click “or create a new one” on the Mashbill row to open it. Change a share, remove an ingredient, press Add an ingredient and start typing to see suggestions, flip Secret, Generic and Partial, and Save to see the new mashbill land in the row behind. Yellow notes are new in this version.

What changed, and what stayed
  • Everything the current popup does is kept: Secret, Generic style and Partial disclosure, the Name that only shows for Secret or Generic, free-text ingredients with suggestions and a colour dot, the total that must land within 99 to 101%, the coloured bar and legend, “Reads as…”, and Notes.
  • The three checkboxes became toggle chips, with their explanations moved into one line under the row that follows what's switched on.
  • Ingredients are a table with a Share column, the same shape as the grapes and Calvados fruit tables. “+ Add an ingredient” adds one blank line (an empty ingredient and an empty %), and suggestions appear under the box as you type, as they do today. Arrow keys and Enter pick one.
  • The popup is 600px (it was 672px), with the title in the serif, a sticky footer, and the page dimmed behind so you can see where the new mashbill will land.
  • Warnings live in the footer, left of Cancel, all in one amber style: over or short of 100%, a missing name or ingredient, and the category rule (Bourbon at least 51% corn, Rye at least 51% rye, Single Malt 100% malted barley). The first kinds stop Save; the category rule is only a warning.
  • To check in the code: the label's mashbill list already filters recipes by category, so a recipe that breaks the rule may not appear in that label's list after you create it. The warning would explain why.
