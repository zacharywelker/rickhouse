What changed
  • Not disclosed is part of the add-distillery row: type a name, or click “or not disclosed” and pick the place. There's no separate checkbox.
  • Dates are dotted-line boxes on the Age row, like “+ mo / days”.
  • “How it's made” (non-chill filtered, additive free, entry proof, peat, colour, char, cooperage) is now one link under “More”.
  • Single barrel and Private selection are under “More” on the bottle, with Notes and “differs from the label”. Picking one reveals its own fields (pick name, barrel number and so on) below it.
