# Rickhouse

Rickhouse records a person's spirits collection and the tastings and tournaments built from it.

## Tournament language

**Tournament**:
A collection of entries played through successive rounds. It begins as a draft and can later be running or complete.

**Entry**:
One participant in a tournament's field: a bottle, a label without an owned bottle, or a named outside pour.

**Round**:
A set of flights played before advancing entries into the next set.

**Flight**:
A group of entries compared within a round. A flight with one entry is a bye.
_Avoid_: Heat in user-facing language

**Bye**:
A flight with one entry that advances without a ranking.
